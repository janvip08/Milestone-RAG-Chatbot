What are the key benefits of the HDFC Top 100 Fund?

HDFC Top 100 Fund is an open-ended equity scheme predominantly investing in large-cap stocks. Key benefits include:

  • Focus on top 100 companies by market capitalization.
  • Potential for long-term capital appreciation.
  • Diversification across sectors within the large-cap universe.

Is there an exit load for this scheme?

Yes, as per the current SID: An exit load of 1% is applicable if units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year from the date of allotment. No exit load is charged for redemption after 1 year.
