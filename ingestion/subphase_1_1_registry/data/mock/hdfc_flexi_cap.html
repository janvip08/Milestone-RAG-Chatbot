Scheme Suitability & Riskometer

This product is suitable for investors who are seeking long-term capital appreciation by investing in equity and equity-related instruments across market capitalizations. Recommended investment horizon: 5+ years.

Expense Ratio

As of April 30, 2026, the Expense Ratio for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund is as follows:

  • Direct Plan: 0.78% per annum
  • Regular Plan: 1.62% per annum

The expense ratio is charged on a daily accrual basis and is reflected in the fund's NAV. It includes management fees, custodian charges, audit fees, and other operational costs.

Exit Load Details

The exit load structure for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund is defined as follows:

  • For redemption/switch-out within 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: 1.00% of the applicable NAV.
  • For redemption/switch-out after 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: Nil.

No exit load is applicable for units allotted through dividend reinvestment or Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) units.

Tax Implications

The tax treatment for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund investments is as per the prevailing Income Tax Act, 1961:

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): Units sold within 12 months are taxed at 20% (plus applicable surcharge and cess).
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): Units sold after 12 months — gains exceeding Rs. 1.25 lakh per financial year are taxed at 12.5% (plus applicable surcharge and cess).
  • Dividend (IDCW) Distribution: Dividends are taxable in the hands of investors at their applicable slab rate. TDS of 10% is deducted for dividends exceeding Rs. 5,000 in a financial year for resident investors.

Risk Factors

Investments in HDFC Flexi Cap Fund are subject to the following risk factors:

  • Market Risk: The fund's NAV is subject to fluctuations based on stock market movements, economic conditions, and global factors.
  • Allocation Risk: As a flexi-cap fund, the portfolio's market-cap allocation may shift significantly, leading to varying risk profiles over time.
  • Concentration Risk: Large holdings in banking and financial services sector may lead to sector-specific risks.
  • Currency Risk: Any international holdings may be affected by currency fluctuations.
  • Regulatory Risk: Changes in SEBI regulations, RBI policies, or government tax laws may impact fund performance.
