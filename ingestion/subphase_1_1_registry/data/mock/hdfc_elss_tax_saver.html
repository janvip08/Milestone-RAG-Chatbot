HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund

AMC: HDFC Asset Management Company Limited

Category: ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) - An open-ended equity linked saving scheme with a statutory lock-in of 3 years and tax benefit under Section 80C.

Net Asset Value (NAV) & Assets Under Management (AUM)

  • Net Asset Value (NAV): As of June 01, 2026, the Net Asset Value (NAV) for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund is Rs. 1,120.50 for the Direct Plan and Rs. 1,015.30 for the Regular Plan.
  • Assets Under Management (AUM): As of April 30, 2026, the Assets Under Management (AUM) for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund is Rs. 15,230 Crores.

Scheme Suitability & Riskometer

This product is suitable for investors who are seeking long-term capital appreciation by investing in equity and equity-related instruments, along with tax benefits. The riskometer for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund is classified as Very High.

Benchmark

The benchmark index for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund is the Nifty 500 TRI (Total Returns Index).

Investment Details & Minimum Amounts

  • Minimum SIP Amount: Rs. 500 per month and in multiples of Rs. 500 thereafter.
  • Minimum Initial Investment (Lumpsum): Rs. 500 and in multiples of Rs. 500 thereafter.
  • Minimum Additional Purchase: Rs. 500 and in multiples of Rs. 500 thereafter.

Lock-in Period

In accordance with the Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) guidelines, investment in HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund is subject to a statutory lock-in period of 3 years (36 months) from the date of allotment. During this lock-in period, investors cannot redeem or switch-out their units.

Expense Ratio

As of April 30, 2026, the Expense Ratio for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund is as follows:

  • Direct Plan: 1.15% per annum
  • Regular Plan: 1.82% per annum

Exit Load Details

The exit load structure for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund is defined as follows:

  • Redemption or Switch-out: Nil (subject to completion of the statutory 3-year lock-in period).
