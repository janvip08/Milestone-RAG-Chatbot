HDFC Focused 30 Fund

AMC: HDFC Asset Management Company Limited

Category: Focused Fund - An open-ended equity scheme investing in maximum 30 stocks (predominantly large cap, mid cap, and small cap companies).

Net Asset Value (NAV) & Assets Under Management (AUM)

  • Net Asset Value (NAV): As of June 01, 2026, the Net Asset Value (NAV) for HDFC Focused 30 Fund is Rs. 420.15 for the Direct Plan and Rs. 385.40 for the Regular Plan.
  • Assets Under Management (AUM): As of April 30, 2026, the Assets Under Management (AUM) for HDFC Focused 30 Fund is Rs. 10,850 Crores.

Scheme Suitability & Riskometer

This product is suitable for investors who are seeking long-term capital appreciation by investing in a focused portfolio of up to 30 companies. The riskometer for HDFC Focused 30 Fund is classified as Very High.

Benchmark

The benchmark index for HDFC Focused 30 Fund is the Nifty 500 TRI (Total Returns Index).

Investment Details & Minimum Amounts

  • Minimum SIP Amount: Rs. 100 per month and in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter.
  • Minimum Initial Investment (Lumpsum): Rs. 100 and in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter.
  • Minimum Additional Purchase: Rs. 100 and in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter.

Expense Ratio

As of April 30, 2026, the Expense Ratio for HDFC Focused 30 Fund is as follows:

  • Direct Plan: 1.05% per annum
  • Regular Plan: 1.78% per annum

Exit Load Details

The exit load structure for HDFC Focused 30 Fund is defined as follows:

  • For redemption/switch-out within 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: 1.00% of the applicable NAV.
  • For redemption/switch-out after 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: Nil.
