HDFC Focused 30 Fund

This product is suitable for investors who are seeking long-term capital appreciation by investing in a focused portfolio of up to 30 companies. The riskometer for HDFC Focused 30 Fund is classified as Very High. Exit Load Details

The exit load structure for HDFC Focused 30 Fund is defined as follows:

  • For redemption/switch-out within 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: 1.00% of the applicable NAV.
  • For redemption/switch-out after 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: Nil.

No exit load is applicable for switch between Growth and IDCW options of the same plan. Exit load may be modified by the AMC with prior notice.

Tax Implications

The tax treatment for HDFC Focused 30 Fund investments is as per the prevailing Income Tax Act, 1961:

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): Units sold within 12 months are taxed at 20% (plus applicable surcharge and cess) as per the latest budget provisions.
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): Units sold after 12 months — gains exceeding Rs. 1.25 lakh per financial year are taxed at 12.5% (plus applicable surcharge and cess).
  • Dividend (IDCW) Distribution: Dividends are taxable in the hands of investors at their applicable slab rate. TDS of 10% is deducted for dividends exceeding Rs. 5,000 in a financial year.
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT): STT of 0.001% is applicable on redemption of equity mutual fund units.

Risk Factors

Investments in HDFC Focused 30 Fund are subject to the following risk factors:

  • Concentration Risk: With a maximum of 30 stocks, the fund is more concentrated than diversified equity funds, leading to potentially higher volatility.
  • Market Risk: NAV may fluctuate due to equity market movements, economic cycles, and geopolitical events.
  • Stock Selection Risk: Performance is highly dependent on the fund manager's stock selection ability.
  • Sector Risk: High allocation to financial services (30.2%) creates sector-specific risk exposure.
  • Regulatory Risk: Changes in SEBI regulations or tax laws may impact the fund's structure and returns.
