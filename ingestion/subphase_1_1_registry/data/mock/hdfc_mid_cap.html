Scheme Suitability & Riskometer

This product is suitable for investors who are seeking long-term capital appreciation by investing predominantly in equity and equity-related instruments of mid-cap companies. The riskometer for HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund is classified as Very High. Investors should have an investment horizon of at least 5-7 years. This fund is not suitable for investors seeking short-term gains or capital preservation.

Exit Load Details

The exit load structure for HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund is defined as follows:

  • For redemption/switch-out within 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: 1.00% of the applicable NAV.
  • For redemption/switch-out after 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: Nil.

Exit load is calculated on the applicable NAV on the date of redemption. No exit load is charged on units allotted via dividend reinvestment.

Tax Implications

The tax treatment for HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund is as per the prevailing Income Tax Act, 1961, as amended from time to time:

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): If units are sold within 12 months of purchase, gains are classified as short-term and taxed at 20% (plus applicable surcharge and cess).
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): If units are sold after 12 months, gains exceeding Rs. 1.25 lakh in a financial year are taxed at 12.5% (plus applicable surcharge and cess) without the benefit of indexation.
  • Dividend Distribution: Dividends (IDCW) are added to the investor's income and taxed at their applicable income tax slab rate. TDS of 10% is deducted if dividends exceed Rs. 5,000 in a financial year.

Risk Factors

Investments in HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund are subject to the following risk factors:

  • Market Risk: NAV may fluctuate due to changes in market conditions, interest rates, and economic factors.

  • Regulatory Risk: Changes in government policies, tax regulations, or SEBI guidelines may impact the fund's operations and returns.
