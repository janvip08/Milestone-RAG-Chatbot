HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund

AMC: HDFC Asset Management Company Limited

Category: Mid Cap Fund - An open-ended equity scheme predominantly investing in mid-cap stocks.

Net Asset Value (NAV) & Assets Under Management (AUM)

  • Net Asset Value (NAV): As of June 01, 2026, the Net Asset Value (NAV) for HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund is Rs. 182.45 for the Direct Plan and Rs. 165.20 for the Regular Plan.
  • Assets Under Management (AUM): As of April 30, 2026, the Assets Under Management (AUM) for HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund is Rs. 65,420 Crores.

Scheme Suitability & Riskometer

This product is suitable for investors who are seeking long-term capital appreciation by investing predominantly in equity and equity-related instruments of mid-cap companies. The riskometer for HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund is classified as Very High.

Benchmark

The benchmark index for HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund is the Nifty Midcap 150 TRI (Total Returns Index).

Investment Details & Minimum Amounts

  • Minimum SIP Amount: Rs. 100 per month and in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter.
  • Minimum Initial Investment (Lumpsum): Rs. 100 and in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter.
  • Minimum Additional Purchase: Rs. 100 and in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter.

Expense Ratio

As of April 30, 2026, the Expense Ratio for HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund is as follows:

  • Direct Plan: 0.85% per annum
  • Regular Plan: 1.55% per annum

Exit Load Details

The exit load structure for HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund is defined as follows:

  • For redemption/switch-out within 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: 1.00% of the applicable NAV.
  • For redemption/switch-out after 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: Nil.
