HDFC Large Cap Fund

AMC: HDFC Asset Management Company Limited

Category: Large Cap Fund - An open-ended equity scheme predominantly investing in large cap stocks.

Fund Type: Open-Ended Equity Scheme

Launch Date: November 10, 1994

Plan Options: Direct Plan and Regular Plan, available in Growth and IDCW (Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal) options.

Also known as: HDFC Top 100 Fund, HDFC Large Cap Fund Direct Plan Growth

Scheme Suitability & Riskometer

This product is suitable for investors who are seeking long-term capital appreciation by investing predominantly in equity and equity-related instruments of large-cap companies (top 100 by market capitalization). The riskometer for HDFC Large Cap Fund is classified as Very High. Large-cap funds are generally considered less volatile than mid-cap and small-cap funds. Suitable for investors with a moderate to high risk appetite and an investment horizon of 3-5 years or more.

Benchmark

The benchmark index for HDFC Large Cap Fund is the Nifty 100 TRI (Total Returns Index). The additional benchmark is the S&P BSE 100 TRI. Previously, the fund was benchmarked against the BSE Sensex.

Investment Details & Minimum Amounts

  • Minimum SIP Amount: Rs. 100 per month and in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter.
  • Minimum Initial Investment (Lumpsum): Rs. 100 and in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter.
  • Minimum Additional Purchase: Rs. 100 and in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter.
  • SIP Frequency Options: Monthly, Quarterly, and Weekly SIP options are available.
  • Systematic Transfer Plan (STP): Available with minimum Rs. 500 per installment.
  • Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP): Available with minimum Rs. 500 per installment.

Expense Ratio

As of April 30, 2026, the Expense Ratio for HDFC Large Cap Fund is as follows:

  • Direct Plan: 1.02% per annum
  • Regular Plan: 1.72% per annum

The TER (Total Expense Ratio) is regulated by SEBI based on the fund's AUM. Larger funds typically have lower TERs due to economies of scale.

Exit Load Details

The exit load structure for HDFC Large Cap Fund is defined as follows:

  • For redemption/switch-out within 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: 1.00% of the applicable NAV.
  • For redemption/switch-out after 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment: Nil.

Tax Implications

Tax treatment for HDFC Large Cap Fund:

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): Units sold within 12 months are taxed at 20% (plus applicable surcharge and cess).
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): Units sold after 12 months — gains exceeding Rs. 1.25 lakh per financial year are taxed at 12.5% (plus applicable surcharge and cess).
  • Dividend (IDCW): Taxable at investor's applicable slab rate. TDS of 10% for dividends exceeding Rs. 5,000 per financial year.
  • STT: Securities Transaction Tax of 0.001% on redemption.

Risk Factors

  • Market Risk: Large-cap stocks, while relatively stable, are still subject to market volatility.
  • Concentration Risk: Heavy allocation to banking sector (32.5%) creates sector-specific risk.
  • Regulatory Risk: Changes in government policies and SEBI regulations may impact returns.
  • Global Risk: Global economic events, trade wars, and geopolitical tensions can impact large-cap companies.
