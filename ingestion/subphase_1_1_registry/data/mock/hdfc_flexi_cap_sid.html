HDFC Flexi Cap Fund - Scheme Information Document (SID)

AMC: HDFC Asset Management Company Limited

SEBI Registration Number: MF/022/95/5

1. Scheme Details

Scheme Name: HDFC Flexi Cap Fund (formerly known as HDFC Equity Fund)

Type: Open-Ended Equity Scheme - Flexi Cap Fund

Investment Objective: To provide long-term capital appreciation by investing in equity and equity-related securities across market capitalizations. The fund manager has flexibility to dynamically allocate between large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap stocks based on prevailing market conditions and valuations.

2. Asset Allocation Pattern

  • Equity and Equity-related instruments across market capitalizations: 65% to 100% (High Risk)
  • Debt Securities, Government Securities, and Money Market Instruments: 0% to 35% (Low to Medium Risk)
  • Units issued by REITs and InvITs: 0% to 10% (Very High Risk)

As a flexi-cap fund, SEBI mandates a minimum 65% allocation to equity instruments but provides flexibility in market-cap allocation. The fund is required to invest a minimum of 65% in equity across large, mid, and small-cap categories without any sub-category minimum.

3. Investment Strategy

The fund employs a multi-cap investment approach with the flexibility to dynamically shift allocations based on market conditions. Key aspects of the strategy include:

  • Bottom-up stock selection combined with top-down macro overlay
  • Focus on businesses with strong competitive moats and pricing power
  • Preference for companies with demonstrated track record of earnings growth
  • Valuation-conscious approach — willingness to hold cash when opportunities are limited
  • Active management of market-cap allocation based on relative valuations
  • Emphasis on quality of earnings and return on capital employed

4. Risk Management

  • Diversification across 50-60 stocks to mitigate single-stock risk
  • Maximum single stock exposure limited to 10% as per SEBI regulations
  • Sector limits monitored to avoid excessive concentration
  • Regular portfolio stress testing under adverse market scenarios
  • Liquidity assessment of holdings to ensure orderly redemption capability

5. Who Should Invest

HDFC Flexi Cap Fund is suitable for investors seeking long-term wealth creation through equity market exposure, who can tolerate short-term volatility, have a minimum 5-year investment horizon, and prefer a diversified equity fund with flexibility across market capitalizations. Not suitable for short-term investors or those seeking guaranteed returns.

  • Additional Purchase: Rs. 100 and in multiples of Re. 1
  • Minimum SIP: Rs. 100 per month

  • Minimum STP: Rs. 500 per installment

10. Tax Treatment

  • STCG (less than 12 months): 20% plus surcharge and cess
  • LTCG (more than 12 months): 12.5% on gains exceeding Rs. 1.25 lakh per FY
  • Dividends: Taxable at slab rate, TDS 10% if exceeding Rs. 5,000/FY
  • STT: 0.001% on redemption
