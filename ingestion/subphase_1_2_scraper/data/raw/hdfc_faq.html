HDFC Mutual Fund - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

General Questions

What is a Mutual Fund?

A mutual fund is a professionally managed investment vehicle that pools money from multiple investors to invest in a diversified portfolio of securities such as stocks, bonds, and money market instruments. Each investor owns units proportional to their investment. Mutual funds are regulated by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and managed by Asset Management Companies (AMCs) like HDFC Asset Management Company Limited.

What is HDFC Mutual Fund?

HDFC Mutual Fund is one of the largest mutual fund houses in India, sponsored by Housing Development Finance Corporation Limited (HDFC Ltd). HDFC Asset Management Company Limited (HDFC AMC) is the investment manager of HDFC Mutual Fund. As of April 2026, HDFC Mutual Fund manages assets worth over Rs. 6.5 lakh crores across equity, debt, hybrid, and other fund categories. HDFC AMC is listed on both BSE and NSE.

What is NAV (Net Asset Value)?

Net Asset Value (NAV) is the per-unit market value of all the securities held by a mutual fund scheme. It is calculated by dividing the total value of all assets in the portfolio (minus liabilities and expenses) by the total number of outstanding units. NAV is computed at the end of every business day. When you invest in a mutual fund, you buy units at the NAV prevailing on the date of purchase (subject to applicable cut-off times). The NAV fluctuates daily based on the market value of the underlying securities.

What is AUM (Assets Under Management)?

Assets Under Management (AUM) refers to the total market value of all investments managed by a mutual fund scheme or the entire AMC. AUM changes based on inflows (new investments), outflows (redemptions), and market movements. A higher AUM generally indicates investor confidence and provides economies of scale in managing the fund, often resulting in lower expense ratios.

Account & Statement Related

How can I download my mutual fund account statement?

You can download your HDFC Mutual Fund account statement through the following methods:

  • HDFC MF Website: Log in to www.hdfcfund.com, go to 'My Account' section, and click on 'Account Statement'. Select the date range and download in PDF format.
  • Email Request: Send an email to cliser@hdfcfund.com with your folio number and PAN, requesting an account statement.
  • CAMS (Registrar): Visit www.camsonline.com, log in with your PAN, and download consolidated statements across all fund houses serviced by CAMS.
  • MF Central: Visit www.mfcentral.com for a consolidated view of all your mutual fund holdings across AMCs.
  • Mobile App: Use the HDFC MF mobile app to view and download statements.

How can I download my capital gains statement?

Your capital gains statement for tax filing can be obtained through:

  • HDFC MF Website: Log in, navigate to 'Reports' > 'Capital Gains Statement'. Select the financial year and download.
  • CAMS: Visit www.camsonline.com > 'Investors' > 'Capital Gain/Loss Statement'. Enter your PAN and select the period.
  • Consolidated Statement: Request a CAS (Consolidated Account Statement) from your depository (NSDL/CDSL) which includes capital gains information.

The capital gains statement includes details of purchase date, purchase NAV, sale date, sale NAV, units transacted, capital gains/losses, and applicable tax category (STCG or LTCG) for each transaction.

How can I update my bank account details?

To update your bank account details in HDFC Mutual Fund:

  • Submit a Bank Mandate Change form along with a cancelled cheque or bank statement at any HDFC MF branch or CAMS service center.
  • Online: Log in to HDFC MF website, go to 'Service Requests' > 'Bank Mandate Change'.
  • The new bank account must be in the name of the first/sole holder.

How can I update my KYC details?

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is mandatory for mutual fund investments. To update KYC:

  • Visit any HDFC MF branch or CAMS service center with valid ID proof and address proof.
  • Online KYC can be done through www.cvlkra.com, www.karvykra.com, or www.camskra.com.
  • Video-based KYC (V-KYC) is available through the HDFC MF app for paperless verification.

Investment & Redemption

What is the difference between Direct Plan and Regular Plan?

Direct Plan: The investor invests directly with the AMC without any intermediary. The NAV is higher and the expense ratio is lower because no distributor commission is paid. Available online through the AMC website or app.

Regular Plan: The investor invests through a distributor or financial advisor. The NAV is lower and the expense ratio is higher because it includes distributor commission. The difference in NAV between Direct and Regular plans reflects the commission paid to distributors.

What is SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)?

SIP allows you to invest a fixed amount at regular intervals (weekly, monthly, or quarterly) in a mutual fund scheme. Benefits of SIP include: rupee cost averaging (buying more units when NAV is low and fewer when high), disciplined investing habit, no need to time the market, and flexibility to start with as low as Rs. 100 per month. SIP can be set up through bank mandate, OTM (One-Time Mandate), or online payment.

What is SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan)?

SWP allows you to withdraw a fixed amount at regular intervals from your mutual fund investment. It provides regular cash flow while keeping the remaining investment growing. Minimum SWP amount is Rs. 500 per installment for most HDFC MF schemes.

What is STP (Systematic Transfer Plan)?

STP allows you to transfer a fixed amount from one HDFC mutual fund scheme to another at regular intervals. It is commonly used to transfer from a liquid/debt fund to an equity fund to gain the benefit of rupee cost averaging.

What are the cut-off times for mutual fund transactions?

For equity and balanced/hybrid funds: If the application and payment are received before 3:00 PM on a business day, the NAV of that day is applicable. If received after 3:00 PM, the next business day's NAV applies. For liquid and overnight funds: The cut-off time is 1:30 PM for subscription and 3:00 PM for redemption.

How long does it take to receive redemption proceeds?

For equity/balanced funds: Within 3 business days from the date of acceptance of the redemption request. For debt/liquid funds: Within 1 business day. The proceeds are credited directly to the registered bank account of the investor.

Tax & Compliance

What are the tax implications of mutual fund investments?

For equity-oriented funds (equity allocation > 65%): Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) on units held for less than 12 months are taxed at 20%. Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) on units held for more than 12 months exceeding Rs. 1.25 lakh per financial year are taxed at 12.5%. For debt-oriented funds: All gains are taxed at the investor's applicable income tax slab rate, regardless of holding period.

Is TDS applicable on mutual fund transactions?

TDS is applicable on dividend (IDCW) payouts exceeding Rs. 5,000 per financial year at the rate of 10% for resident investors. TDS is not applicable on capital gains from mutual fund redemptions for resident investors (they must declare and pay tax through their income tax returns).
