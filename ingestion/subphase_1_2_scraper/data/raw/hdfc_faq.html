HDFC Mutual Fund Help Center

How do I download my Account Statement?

You can download your account statement by following these steps:

  1. Go to the official HDFC Mutual Fund Investor Portal (https://www.hdfcfund.com).
  2. Log in using your PAN/Email/Folio Number and OTP/Password.
  3. Navigate to 'My Portfolio' and select 'Account Statements'.
  4. Choose the specific Folio Number and financial period.
  5. Click 'Download PDF' or select 'Request via Email' to receive it on your registered email address.

Alternatively, you can request an account statement via SMS by sending 'HDFCMF STMT ' to 9212900022.

How do I download my Capital Gains Report?

To download your Capital Gains statement for tax filing purposes, follow these steps:

  1. Visit the HDFC Mutual Fund customer portal.
  2. Click on 'Transact Online' or log in to your account.
  3. Select 'Statements' from the side menu, then click on 'Capital Gains Statement'.
  4. Select the relevant Financial Year (e.g., FY 2025-26).
  5. Click on 'Generate Report' and download the statement in PDF or Excel format.

You can also download consolidated capital gains reports across multiple AMCs via the CAMS or KFintech registrar websites using your registered PAN.
