HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund - Scheme Information Document (SID)

AMC: HDFC Asset Management Company Limited

SEBI Registration Number: MF/022/95/5

1. Scheme Details

Scheme Name: HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund

Type: Open-Ended Equity Scheme - Mid Cap Fund

Investment Objective: To provide long-term capital appreciation by investing predominantly in equity and equity-related instruments of mid-cap companies. The fund aims to generate returns that are commensurate with the risk profile of the scheme. There is no assurance that the investment objective of the scheme will be achieved.

2. Asset Allocation Pattern

The scheme shall invest as follows:

  • Equity and Equity-related instruments of Mid Cap companies: 65% to 100% (High Risk)
  • Equity and Equity-related instruments of companies other than Mid Cap: 0% to 35% (High Risk)
  • Debt Securities, Government Securities, and Money Market Instruments: 0% to 35% (Low to Medium Risk)
  • Units issued by REITs and InvITs: 0% to 10% (Very High Risk)

Mid-cap companies are defined as companies ranking 101st to 250th in terms of full market capitalization as per AMFI classification. The fund will maintain a minimum of 65% allocation to mid-cap stocks at all times as mandated by SEBI.

3. Investment Strategy

The fund follows a bottom-up stock selection approach. The fund manager identifies companies with strong growth potential, good corporate governance, capable management teams, and sustainable competitive advantages. The investment strategy involves:

  • Thorough fundamental analysis including assessment of business models, earnings quality, balance sheet strength, and cash flow generation
  • Evaluation of management quality and corporate governance practices
  • Assessment of competitive positioning and market share dynamics
  • Valuation discipline with focus on margin of safety
  • Sector allocation as an outcome of individual stock selection

4. Risk Management Framework

The fund employs a comprehensive risk management framework:

  • Portfolio diversification across sectors and stocks to mitigate concentration risk
  • Liquidity management through maintaining adequate liquid assets
  • Regular stress testing of the portfolio under various market scenarios
  • Compliance with SEBI-mandated single issuer limit (10%) and sector concentration limits
  • Monitoring of tracking error versus the benchmark index
  • Daily VaR (Value at Risk) calculations
  • Regular review of credit quality for any debt holdings

5. Who Should Invest

This scheme is suitable for investors who: seek long-term capital appreciation over a period of 5 to 7 years or more, can tolerate high levels of volatility in the short to medium term, want exposure to mid-cap companies with growth potential, and have an investment objective aligned with wealth creation through equity markets. Investors with short-term investment horizons, low risk tolerance, or capital preservation objectives should not invest in this scheme.

7. Benchmark Information

The benchmark for HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund is the Nifty Midcap 150 Total Returns Index (TRI). The fund's performance is compared against this benchmark on a regular basis across 1-year, 3-year, 5-year, and since-inception periods. The benchmark was changed from Nifty Midcap 100 TRI to Nifty Midcap 150 TRI effective from December 2021, as per SEBI guidelines.

8. Expenses, Loads, and Charges

  • Entry Load: Not applicable (as per SEBI circular dated June 30, 2009, no entry load is charged for mutual fund schemes)
  • Exit Load: 1.00% if redeemed/switched out within 365 days from the date of allotment; Nil after 365 days
  • Total Expense Ratio (Direct Plan): 0.85% per annum
  • Total Expense Ratio (Regular Plan): 1.55% per annum
  • Transaction Charges: Rs. 100 for existing investors and Rs. 150 for first-time investors, for investments of Rs. 10,000 or more through a distributor

9. Minimum Investment Requirements

  • Minimum Application Amount (New Investors / Lumpsum): Rs. 100 and in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter
  • Minimum Additional Investment: Rs. 100 and in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter
  • Minimum SIP Investment: Rs. 100 per month
  • Minimum SWP Amount: Rs. 500 per installment
  • Minimum STP Amount: Rs. 500 per installment

10. Tax Treatment for Investors

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (units held less than 12 months): Taxed at 20% plus applicable surcharge and cess
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (units held more than 12 months): Gains exceeding Rs. 1.25 lakh per financial year taxed at 12.5% plus applicable surcharge and cess
  • Dividend Distribution: Dividends are taxed in the hands of investors at their applicable income tax slab rate. TDS of 10% for dividends exceeding Rs. 5,000 in a financial year
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT): 0.001% on redemption of equity mutual fund units
