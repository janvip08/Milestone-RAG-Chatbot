Lock-in Period & Tax Benefits

HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund has a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of allotment of each unit. This is the shortest lock-in among all tax-saving investment options under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

Tax Deduction under Section 80C: Investments up to Rs. 1,50,000 per financial year in HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund qualify for deduction under Section 80C. This reduces the investor's taxable income, leading to tax savings of up to Rs. 46,800 (at the highest tax bracket of 30% + 4% cess).

Comparison with other 80C options: ELSS has the shortest lock-in (3 years) compared to PPF (15 years), NSC (5 years), Tax Saving FD (5 years), and NPS (till retirement). ELSS also offers the potential for higher returns through equity market exposure.

Investment Details & Minimum Amounts

  • Minimum SIP Amount: Rs. 500 per month and in multiples of Rs. 500 thereafter.
  • Minimum Initial Investment (Lumpsum): Rs. 500 and in multiples of Rs. 500 thereafter.
  • Minimum Additional Purchase: Rs. 500 and in multiples of Rs. 500 thereafter.
  • Maximum Investment for 80C benefit: Rs. 1,50,000 per financial year (combined limit under Section 80C).
  • SIP Frequency Options: Monthly and Quarterly SIP options are available.
  • Important: Each SIP installment is treated as a fresh investment and has its own 3-year lock-in period.

Exit Load Details

The exit load for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund is Nil. Since the scheme has a mandatory 3-year lock-in period, no exit load is applicable upon redemption after the lock-in period. Units cannot be redeemed before the completion of the 3-year lock-in period from the date of allotment of each unit.

Tax Implications

  • Section 80C Deduction: Investment up to Rs. 1,50,000 per financial year is eligible for deduction from taxable income under Section 80C.
  • Lock-in: Mandatory 3-year lock-in from allotment date of each unit.
  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): Not applicable as units cannot be sold within 12 months due to the 3-year lock-in.
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): Gains exceeding Rs. 1.25 lakh per financial year are taxed at 12.5% (plus applicable surcharge and cess) upon redemption after the lock-in period.
  • Dividend (IDCW): Taxable at investor's slab rate. TDS of 10% for dividends exceeding Rs. 5,000.

Risk Factors

  • Lock-in Risk: Units cannot be redeemed for 3 years, even in case of market downturns or personal liquidity needs.
  • Market Risk: Being an equity fund, NAV is subject to market volatility.
  • Concentration Risk: Large allocation to financial services sector.
  • Tax Policy Risk: Future changes in Section 80C limits or ELSS tax treatment may impact the tax benefit proposition.
